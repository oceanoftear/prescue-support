PresCue 支援與常見問題

PresCue Support · 聯絡我們: digitimpact@gmail.com(中文/English)

刪除帳號與資料

在 App 內即可自行刪除:帳號頁 → 刪除帳號。刪除後,帳號、講稿、配音與所有雲端資料將被永久移除,無法復原。

You can delete your account and all associated data in the app: Account page → Delete Account. This permanently removes your account, scripts, dubbing files and all cloud data.

練習要付費嗎?

不用。聽稿、綵排、上台全功能用手機內建語音即可無限練習,永久免費。AI 母語配音按份購買,註冊即送 1 份完整配音。

No. Listen, rehearse and on-stage features are free forever with system voices. Pro AI dubbing is a one-time per-speech purchase; every new account gets one full AI dub free.

我的講稿安全嗎?

語音辨識在你的裝置上進行。上傳的簡報在轉換完成後立即刪除原檔;雲端素材 30 天後自動清除。詳見 隱私權政策。

Speech recognition runs on your device. Uploaded files are deleted right after conversion, and cloud artifacts auto-expire after 30 days. See the privacy policy.

回報問題

來信 digitimpact@gmail.com,附上 iOS 版本與操作步驟,我們會盡快回覆。

Email digitimpact@gmail.com with your iOS version and steps to reproduce; we usually reply within 1–2 days.
